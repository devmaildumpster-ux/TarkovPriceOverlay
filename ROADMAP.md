# Roadmap

What's coming to Tarkov Price Overlay. No dates, just what's being worked on and what's planned next.
Ideas and feedback are welcome: open an issue.

## Done

- **Update check on launch + GitHub Releases** (1.2.6). When the app starts, it checks the latest release on GitHub
  and shows a small "Update available" strip with a Download button if there's a newer version. It never installs
  anything by itself and stays quiet when you're offline. New versions are published as
  [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/devmaildumpster-ux/TarkovPriceOverlay/releases). *Status: Done*

## Planned

### High priority

- **Ammo chart.** A hotkey popup for the caliber you're hovering that compares every ammo type side by side
  (penetration, damage and armor damage), so you can tell rounds apart at a glance.
  *Status: Planned*

### Prices and items

- **Icon short-name tiebreaker.** Read the item's short name from its icon, so "Possible match" cards resolve on
  their own instead of asking you to check. *Status: Planned*
- **Quest & hideout flags.** Mark items you still need for quests or hideout upgrades, e.g. "Needed: 2 for
  Gunsmith". *Status: Planned*
- **Barter & craft value.** Show when bartering or crafting with an item is worth more than selling it on the flea.
  *Status: Planned*
- **Price trend.** A small 24-hour / 7-day trend arrow on cards, so you can see whether to sell now or hold.
  *Status: Planned*
- **Live inventory value.** A running tally of what your current inventory is worth that updates live as you add
  and remove items. *Status: Planned*

### Map

- **Quest objectives and locked rooms.** Show quest objectives and locked rooms on the 2D map, with the key each
  room needs and what that key costs. *Status: Planned*
- **Extract requirements.** Show what each of your extracts needs: paying, a switch, no backpack and so on.
  *Status: Planned*

### App

- **PvP / PvE profile switch.** Switch between PvP and PvE prices without opening Settings. *Status: Planned*
- **Crash & error report button.** An opt-in button that saves a diagnostic file you can share when something
  goes wrong. Nothing is ever uploaded automatically. *Status: Planned*
