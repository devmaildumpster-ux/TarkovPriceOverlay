Tarkov Price Overlay 1.4.0 — Controls & hotkeys (default keys)
In the app: the "? Controls" button at the top shows this list with YOUR current keys.
Shortcuts can be changed or cleared in Settings… (Overlay & Card: price shortcut on/off; Overlay & Card / Map & Screenshots: Record… / Clear / Default).
Run Tarkov in Borderless or Windowed mode (cards can't draw over exclusive Fullscreen).

PRICE CARDS (IN GAME)
  Rest the mouse on an item     Auto-read on hover (on by default): when Tarkov's name tooltip appears (after
                                350 ms), the price card shows by itself.
  ` (backtick)                  Price shortcut: hover an item until its name tooltip shows, then press it for
                                the price card next to the mouse.
  Same key again                With a card showing (mouse still): expand / collapse the full list of quests,
                                crafts, barters and hideout needs.
  Ctrl+` / Ctrl+Shift+F8        Used as the price shortcut instead of the backtick if another app already has
                                it (the top bar shows which one you got).
  Move the mouse                Hides the card (past 6 px). It also hides after 7 s (15 s when expanded). The
                                card never takes focus; clicks go through it.
  Ctrl+Alt+W                    Open the Tarkov Wiki page of the last item looked up.
  Ctrl+Alt+D                    Open the last item looked up on tarkov.dev.
  Ctrl+Alt+A                    Open the ammo chart for the caliber you last hovered or looked up. Every round
                                of that caliber is compared side by side (penetration, damage, armor classes,
                                prices). Esc closes it. Also Ammo… in the main window.

QUICK SEARCH BAR (IN GAME)
  Alt+`                         Open / close the search bar over the game.
  Type                          Filter by item name or short name.
  ↑ / ↓                         Pick a result.
  Enter or double-click         Show that item's price card.
  Esc                           Close the bar and give the keyboard back to the game.
  Click outside the bar         Closes it.

MAP
  Ctrl+Alt+M                    Switch the map Satellite ↔ Vector from in game (never takes focus; docked or
                                popped out).
  Satellite / Vector button     Same switch on the map toolbar (greyed out on maps that have only one style).
  Mouse wheel                   Zoom in / out at the mouse.
  Left-drag                     Pan the map.
  Double-click the map          Fit the whole map (also More ▾ › Fit whole map).
  Hover a pin                   Show the exit / transit / quest spot name and details.
  Map box                       Pick a map. Auto-follow (tick box) switches to the raid's map by itself.
  Floor box                     Pick a floor / underground level. Auto layer switching from screenshots: All
                                floors & underground (Settings… › Map & Screenshots); a floor you pick stays
                                until your next screenshot.
  Exits / Quests / Me           Show or hide exits, your picked quests and your position dot.
  Pop out / Dock                Open the map in its own window (remembers monitor and size), or put it back.
  F11 or Fullscreen             In the popped-out map: borderless fullscreen on that monitor.
  Esc                           Leave fullscreen (popped-out map).

SCREENSHOTS & POSITION (IN RAID)
  PrtScn                        Tarkov's own Screenshot key (Tarkov Settings › Controls). Each screenshot puts
                                your dot and facing on the map, and switches the floor. Bind it if nothing
                                happens.
  More ▾ › Test position from latest screenshot
                                Re-reads the newest screenshot and says what happened (folder, file,
                                position).
  O                             Experimental exit-list reader (off by default; Settings… › Map & Screenshots):
                                after you press O in raid it reads the exit list and fades and crosses out (⊗)
                                the map's exits that aren't on it.

BUTTONS, MENUS & WINDOWS
  Ammo…                         Opens the ammo chart: pick a caliber and compare every round (penetration,
                                damage, armor classes 1–6, flea and trader prices for this PvP/PvE mode).
                                Shortcut: the ammo-chart key above. Also More ▾ › Ammo chart….
  ? Controls                    This list (also More ▾, the tray menu and Settings…). Shows your current keys.
  Settings…                     Settings (also click the PvP/PvE badge): Overlay & Card, Map & Screenshots,
                                Troubleshooting. Changes apply right away.
  Settings… › Record… / Clear / Default
                                Change a shortcut: click Record… and press the new keys (Esc cancels), Clear
                                to turn it off, Default to restore it.
  Refresh prices                Download fresh prices now.
  More ▾ (map)                  Exits for auto / PMC / Scav, Show transits, Browse all quests, Area names, Fit
                                whole map, Quest tracker…, Hideout tracker…, Ammo chart…, Test position…, Map
                                settings…, Controls & hotkeys.
  Quests…                       Quest tracker. Double-click or Space marks a quest done / not done.
  Hideout…                      Hideout tracker: the station levels you've built (cards skip those needs).
  Deselect all / Only my active quests
                                Quick quest-pin choices under the map.
  Pop out card / Load price history / Wiki / tarkov.dev
                                Buttons under the item card in the main window.
  Popped-out card               Drag to move; double-click or Esc closes it.
  − / + / ×                     Tracked items: one handed in, one more, stop tracking.
  Update available: Update now / Release page / Skip this version / ×
                                Shows under the top bar when a newer version is on GitHub. Update now
                                downloads it from GitHub, checks it, installs it and restarts the overlay
                                (settings kept; Cancel stops the download); Release page opens it in your
                                browser instead; Skip hides that version; × hides it until next launch. Also
                                in Settings… › Troubleshooting › Updates.

TRAY ICON (WHEN MINIMIZE TO TRAY IS ON)
  Click / double-click          Show the main window.
  Right-click                   Menu: Open, Settings…, Controls & hotkeys, Quit.
