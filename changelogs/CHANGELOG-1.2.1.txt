Tarkov Price Overlay 1.2.1 (applies over 1.0.0 – 1.2.0, self-contained build)

INSTALL
1. Close Tarkov Price Overlay (if "Minimize to tray" is on: right-click the tray icon › Quit).
2. Copy every file and folder in this zip (including "licenses") into
   <your install folder>\TarkovPriceOverlay\self-contained
   and choose "Replace the files in the destination". Copy everything, not just the exe.
3. Start TarkovPriceOverlay.exe (overlay.log / smoke test says 1.2.1.0).
   Settings, tracked items, quest progress and map settings are kept.

NEW: SATELLITE MAP
- The map now uses tarkov.dev's rendered satellite tiles by default wherever they exist:
  Customs, Woods, Shoreline, Interchange, Reserve, Factory, Ground Zero, The Lab, The Labyrinth, Icebreaker.
  Streets of Tarkov, Lighthouse and Terminal only have the vector drawing, so they stay vector.
- Sharper: the satellite picture is built at tile zoom 4 (about 4000 px across; 1.2.0 used about 2000 px),
  e.g. Customs 4352×2560, Woods 4352×4352, Ground Zero 3072×4096. Icebreaker uses zoom 3 (2304×4096).
  The first open of a map downloads its tiles (5–25 MB, about 5–8 s); after that it opens from the cache.
- Floors: in Satellite the floor's tiles are drawn over the dimmed ground level (like tarkov.dev). A floor that
  exists in only one style is shown in that style (Interchange/Shoreline/Streets floors: vector; Customs 4th
  floor and Reserve's upper floors: satellite).
- Exits, transits, quest pins, area names and your dot use the same projection in both styles. Checked against
  the pictures on Customs and Ground Zero: buildings, roads and pins line up.
- Area names and exit labels on the satellite picture sit on dark rounded backdrops so they read well.
  Credit in the corner: "Map © Tarkov.dev / Shebuka · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0" (author per map).

NEW: SWITCH WITHOUT ALT-TAB
- Satellite / Vector button on the map toolbar (the same button in the docked map and the pop-out window).
  On a map that has only one style the button is greyed out and says why (e.g. "Streets of Tarkov is vector-only").
- Global shortcut Ctrl+Alt+M switches Satellite ↔ Vector from inside the game. It never activates or focuses the
  overlay, so Tarkov keeps the keyboard, and it works with the map docked or popped out on another monitor.
  A short hint ("Map: Satellite" / "Map: Vector") shows in the top-left of the map.
- Settings… › Map & Screenshots: "Default to the satellite map where available" (on) and the shortcut
  (Record… / Clear / Default). The button's tooltip shows the current shortcut.

ALSO

TEST STEPS
1. Open Customs: the map is the satellite picture, the toolbar button says "Satellite".
2. Zoom into Dorms: trees, roofs and roads are crisp; labels have dark backdrops; exits sit on the gates.
3. In game, press Ctrl+Alt+M: the map switches to Vector (hint "Map: Vector") and Tarkov keeps focus.
   Press again: back to Satellite. Pop the map out to the second monitor and try again.
4. Pick Streets of Tarkov: the button is greyed out; Ctrl+Alt+M shows "Streets of Tarkov is vector-only".
5. Settings… › Map & Screenshots: untick "Default to the satellite map" → the map switches to Vector and stays
   that way after a restart.
