Tarkov Price Overlay 1.2.2 (applies over 1.0.0 – 1.2.1, self-contained build)

INSTALL
1. Close Tarkov Price Overlay (if "Minimize to tray" is on: right-click the tray icon › Quit).
2. Copy every file and folder in this zip (including "licenses") into
   <your install folder>\TarkovPriceOverlay\self-contained
   and choose "Replace the files in the destination". Copy everything, not just the exe.
3. Start TarkovPriceOverlay.exe (overlay.log / smoke test says 1.2.2.0).
   Settings, tracked items, quest progress and map settings are kept.

NEW: THE MAP SWITCHES FLOORS FROM YOUR SCREENSHOT
- Tarkov puts your height (y) in each screenshot's file name. The map now uses it, together with tarkov.dev's
  floor outlines ("extents"), to show the floor you're on, the same way tarkov.dev picks a floor for a marker:
  height from the bottom of the band up to (not including) the top, inside one of the floor's boxes, and the
  first floor in tarkov.dev's order wins. Anywhere else is the main map.
- Underground: Reserve Bunkers (storage, command bunkers, D2, dome tunnels, E1), Customs Underground (ZB-1011,
  ZB-1012, ZB-013, old gas station, switch and boiler basements), Factory Tunnels, Ground Zero Garage and
  underpass, Streets Underground (incl. the centre parking and Concordia garage), Shoreline Underground (resort
  west wing and admin basements), Labs Technical, Icebreaker lower decks.
- Upper floors: Customs dorms / boiler / warehouse 17 / oil rig floors, Reserve buildings and the dome, Factory,
  Ground Zero, Streets, Shoreline resort, Interchange mall, Labs Second Level, Icebreaker decks.
- Take a screenshot in a bunker → the Floor box changes to "Bunkers" and a short "Layer: Bunkers" hint shows.
  Back on the surface → it goes back to Main, and back to the satellite picture if you use satellite.
  Floors tarkov.dev only has as a drawing say so: "Layer: Underground (vector only)".
- Picking a floor yourself in the Floor box is kept until your next screenshot.
- Works with the map docked or popped out. It never activates or focuses the overlay, so Tarkov keeps the keyboard.
- Settings… › Map & Screenshots › "Auto-switch map layer on screenshot":
  All floors & underground (default) · Underground only · Off (manual).
  "Map › Test position from latest screenshot" also switches the floor.

ALSO
- maps.json fallback now keeps tarkov.dev's floor outlines (it is used only when tarkov.dev can't be reached;
  the downloaded maps.json already had them).
- Includes everything from 1.0.1-1.2.1.

TEST STEPS
1. Open Reserve, go into a bunker in raid and press your screenshot key: the Floor box says "Bunkers",
   the hint says "Layer: Bunkers" and your dot is on the bunker drawing.
2. Go back up and take another screenshot: the floor goes back to Main (satellite).
3. Pick "3rd Floor" yourself: it stays until the next screenshot.
4. Settings… › Map & Screenshots: set "Underground only": climbing a building no longer changes the floor,
   bunkers still do. "Off (manual)": nothing changes by itself.
